Software that we use to time and that is available to other timers.

What's Included?

  • One single-computer license to PhotoFinishPro Fully Automatic Timing with update support.
  • One linescan camera, and one USB webcamera
  • The PhotoFinishPro RaceStart Online Server, FREE access with purchase.
  • Priority email response times from us (within 36 hours during meet season, 24 during off season).

What you need:

  • A Windows 11 PC.
  • Your wireless start device (phone or tablet) to be on the same network as the timing computer. We recommend using your own router or a personal hotspot.

What you need to know:

  • We recommend using a primary and backup system. With that in mind, we have a discount for a bundled system with two timing kits.
  • Our software is developed for TF, we are increasing XC functionality before next XC season. At this time, XC support is limited.
